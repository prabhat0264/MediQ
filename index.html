How It Works

Your appointment in four simple steps

From registration to walking into the consultation room — the same smooth flow for every visit.

01

Create your account

Register as a patient with your basic health details — no paperwork at the OPD counter ever again.

02

Choose a doctor & slot

Browse specialists by department, view their availability, and pick a date and time that suits you.

03

Get your queue token

Receive a unique token instantly. One token per day keeps things fair and your spot secure.

04

Walk in on time

Track your live queue position from home. Arrive only when your turn is approaching — no waiting.
